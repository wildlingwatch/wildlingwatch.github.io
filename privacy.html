Privacy policy

Wildling Watch is built to need as little information about you as possible. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. This page explains what the game does store and why.

What we store

  • An anonymous guest account. When you first play, the game creates a random account ID so your progress and purchases can be saved. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Your game progress, such as stars, wildlings caught, cosmetics owned and settings. It is saved in your browser and, when online saving is available, on our server under your guest account.
  • Purchase records: what you bought, the amount, the currency, the date and Stripe's payment reference. We keep these to deliver items, handle refunds and meet tax and accounting rules.
  • Duel names. If you play a 1v1 duel, the name you type is shown to the other player in that duel.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe on its own secure checkout page. Your card details go straight to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us. Stripe may send you a receipt by email and processes your information under the Stripe Privacy Policy.

Services we use

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Supabase stores guest accounts, saved progress and purchase records, and relays live duels.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts; your browser downloads them from Google.

Like any website, these services receive basic technical information such as your IP address when your browser connects to them.

How long we keep it

Game progress is kept while your guest account is in use. Purchase records are kept as long as the law requires for accounting, which is usually several years.

Your choices

You can clear the game's data from your browser at any time. To have your server-side progress deleted, or to ask what we hold about you, email nagooyen0@gmail.com. Purchase records we are legally required to keep can't be deleted early.

Children

The game doesn't ask for personal information, and we don't knowingly collect it from children. Players under 18 should get a parent or guardian's permission before buying anything.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
