Everything in the full game

Six elemental starters, three rare wildlings you can only catch, and a team that grows with every trail.

🌱

Catch and evolve

Weaken wild ones and throw a seed pod. Evolve at levels 5, 10 and 15, choosing a utility or damage ability each time.

🗺️

Three campaigns

12 trails, some with branching routes, on Easy, Normal and Hard. Each difficulty has its own stars, badges and crown rewards.

∞

Endless mode

The waves never stop. Titans arrive every 10th wave, and your best wave is kept for every trail and difficulty.

👑

Boss Drop

Challenge the Thorn King, Storm Wyrm or Magmaw. Win a bronze, silver or gold chest with gems, cosmetics or a boss-only look.

⚔️

1v1 duels

Share a room code with a friend and race to survive. Send swarms and alphas down your rival's trail.

✨

Cosmetics and shinies

Skins and accessories for every wildling, map themes and orbs. Spot a 1-in-200 shiny and catch it to keep its colors.

A closer look

Every screen is drawn live in your browser, from the trails to every outfit.
