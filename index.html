A creature-catching tower defense

Hold the trail. Protect the Grove.

Station elemental wildlings along winding trails, catch the wild ones that wander in, and evolve your team down utility or damage paths. Clear the campaign, then see how long you last in Endless.

How to play

  1. Station wildlingsPick a wildling from your party and tap open grass beside the trail. Each type has its own ability.
  2. Start the waveWild ones follow the trail toward the Grove. Each one that slips through costs Grove health.
  3. Catch new speciesThrow a seed pod at a weakened wild one. A catch unlocks that species, including the rare Lumi.
  4. Train and evolveKnockouts earn XP. At Lv 5, 10 and 15, pay to evolve and choose a utility or a damage ability.

Duel rules

  • You and your rival each defend your own copy of the Meadow Trail on Normal. Upgrades are switched off and the waves come in the same order for both of you, so it's a fair fight.
  • Waves start on their own. Build fast.
  • Spend berries in the Duel panel to send a Swarm, a Brute or an Alpha down your rival's trail. They get tougher as your wave count rises.
  • The first Grove to fall loses. Leaving a match counts as a loss. A win earns 15 gems and 30 pass XP.
  • No account needed: just open the game, create a duel and send your friend the code.

Field notes

  • Wildlings gain XP from knockouts. At Lv 5, 10 and 15 they stop leveling until you pay to evolve them. Each evolution lets you pick a utility or a damage ability, and brings a new look and much stronger stats. Tap the i on a party card to preview the choices.
  • Throw an orb at a weakened wild one to catch it. Lower health means better odds. A catch unlocks that species and gives you one free to station.
  • Campaign: clear 12 trails, each with its own map (the last four branch into several routes, and wild ones take turns choosing one), for up to 3 stars each. Spend stars on permanent upgrades. Endless tracks your best wave.
  • Open Trails & upgrades to visit the Cosmetics: skins and accessories for each wildling, map themes and orb styles, bought with gems you earn by playing.
  • Saving: campaign runs save after every cleared wave. Use Save & quit between waves, then pick it up again from Saved runs in Trails & upgrades. Endless runs are not saved.
  • From wave 3 on, some wild ones arrive as special kinds (Swift, Armored, Mender, Splitter, Burrower, Warden and Phantom), each needing a different plan. The home page lists them all.
  • Every 5th wave brings an alpha. Letting one through costs 5 Grove.
  • Every 5th wave has a 60% chance of a glowing rare wildling: Lumi (most common), Gloom or Rime. Miss two in a row and the next 5th wave is guaranteed one. They won't hurt the Grove, but they escape if they reach the end. Rares can't be bought, only caught, and releasing one returns it to your party.
  • Each stationed wildling has its own targeting: First, Last, Strong, Weak or Close. Set it in the panel above.
  • Keys: M mute, 1–9 pick, Space start wave, C orb, T cycle targeting, Esc cancel.
